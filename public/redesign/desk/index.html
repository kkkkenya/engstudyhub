BAND 01 · TODAY'S MISSION

Pass clean with structured daily focus.

Every morning, Study Desk analyzes your upcoming exam timetable and prepares your daily study mission. Tracks active study streaks, weekly dedicated hours, and urgent past-paper questions.

  • ✓ Dynamic 1-Week exam mode adjustment
  • ✓ Active 5-day streak keeper telemetry
  • ✓ Direct one-click task jump to Practice band
BAND 02 · EXAM RADAR

Predictive past-paper frequency matrix.

Aggregates historical questions and marks. When your lecturer drops a hint, pin it in the matrix to apply a 2.5× weighting multiplier so your revision focuses on what counts.

  • ✓ 10-year historical topic occurrence rates
  • ✓ 2.5× lecturer hint weighting multiplier
  • ✓ 1-Page A4 formula triage sheet generation
BAND 03 · BREAKPOINT CHECKPOINTS

Diagnostic checkpoints, not just answers.

When you miss an engineering problem, Study Desk walks you through intermediate checkpoints (Te, Zp, Diameter) and logs whether the slip was conceptual, modulus choice, or basic algebra.

  • ✓ Accepts any valid SI unit conversion (MPa, Pa, kNm)
  • ✓ Clickable "Broke Here" telemetry tracking
  • ✓ Differentiates Setup vs Modulus vs Sign errors
BAND 04 · DEEP CONTEXT PACKS

Zero-hallucination AI exam triage.

One-click compilation of your syllabus notes, lecturer slides, identified student weakspots, and past-paper diagrams into a structured Markdown prompt pack (~24,500 tokens) ready for Claude or DeepSeek.

  • ✓ Grounded in real Kenyan past papers and CATs
  • ✓ One-click clipboard copy for LLM reasoning models
  • ✓ Zero hallucinated references or formulas

SETUP IN 3 STEPS

Get Started in Under 5 Minutes.

01

Download StudyDesk.exe

Double-click the downloaded binary installer. It self-extracts automatically to %LOCALAPPDATA%\StudyDesk with zero administrative approval required.

02

Get Your Key in Discord

Open the Discord server and type /study link. The bot generates your private single-use key (e.g. SD-7K2M-9QXA).

03

Run setup.bat & Study

Paste your code, select your units, and Study Desk opens in Edge App mode at localhost:5190.
