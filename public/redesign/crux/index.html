Crux

Crux is a study app for KU engineering units. It takes the past papers and books for your units and turns them into practice questions. When you get one wrong, it helps you find the step where your working went off.

Practice questions from your papers and books

  • Past papers and CATs, split into questions with each part on its own line.
  • The worked examples and exercises in your unit's books, word for word, with the book's own solution kept as the answer.
  • Open in book takes you to the page the question is on.

Worked solutions

  • Type your final answer and Crux checks it. If it's wrong, you pick the step where your working went off, and that step is saved as a weak spot.
  • Ask for the full solution: what's given, the method, numbered steps with the maths typeset, and a check at the end.
  • Beams and bars come with a free body diagram drawn to scale.

A study guide for each topic

  • What the course expects you to be able to do, the formulas, and the chapter, section and pages to read in your own books.
  • What you can skip, how the topic tends to be examined, and an order to study it in.
  • Each guide is made once and shared with your whole class.

Notes and a focus timer

  • Select a formula or a step anywhere in Crux and pin it. It stays in the notes bar while you work, and you can copy it into any AI.
  • A focus timer you can run alone or in a unit room with others studying the same unit.
  • There are also timed papers, a Prompt Builder for ChatGPT or Claude, and your work syncs between your phone and laptop.
