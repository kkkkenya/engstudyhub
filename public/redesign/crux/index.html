Crux.

A study app for KU engineering units. It turns your unit's past papers and books into practice questions and shows the step where your working went wrong.

Sign in with Discord Join the Hub

Included with Basic and Premium, and in the 7-day free trial when you join. On a computer, Discord shows a QR code you can scan with the Discord app on your phone.

1

Questions from your papers and your books

  • Past papers and CATs, split into questions with each part on its own line.
  • The worked examples and exercises in your unit's books, word for word, with the book's own solution kept as the answer.
  • Open in book takes you to the page the question is on.
2

Worked solutions you can follow

  • Type your final answer and Crux checks it. If it's wrong, you pick the step where your working went off, and that step is saved as a weak spot.
  • Ask for the full solution: what's given, the method, numbered steps with the maths typeset, and a check at the end.
  • Beams and bars come with a free body diagram drawn to scale.
3

What to read for each topic

  • A study guide per topic: what the course expects you to do, the formulas, and the chapter, section and pages in your own books.
  • What to skip, how the topic is examined, and an order to study it in.
  • Made once and shared with your class.
4

Notes on screen, and a timer

  • Select a formula or a step anywhere in Crux and pin it. It stays in the notes bar while you work, ready to copy into any AI.
  • A focus timer, alone or in a unit room with others studying the same unit.
  • Also: timed papers, a Prompt Builder for ChatGPT or Claude, and everything synced between your phone and laptop.
